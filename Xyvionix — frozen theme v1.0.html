The state model

Assurance is a state, not a report

Every system under assurance holds one of four states, determined by evidence against agreed thresholds. The state is current, not historical — and it is legible without colour: solid means proven, hollow means breached.

  1. Assured

    Current evidence proves the outcome is being met within threshold.

  2. Degrading

    Evidence shows movement toward the threshold. Corrective action begins here.

  3. At risk

    Threshold breached. The outcome can no longer be proven as agreed.

  4. Unassured

    No current evidence exists. The system’s condition is unknown, not acceptable.

The assurance engine

The intelligence layer beneath continuous assurance

Signals from the estate become evidence. Evidence is tested against thresholds. Thresholds determine state. State that falls short triggers corrective action, which closes only when closure is verified.

  1. Signals

    Telemetry, configuration, cost and usage drawn from the estate.

  2. Evidence

    Tested, retained and attributable to a point in time.

  3. Thresholds

    Agreed with the business, not set by engineering convention.

  4. State

    Current rather than historical. Held, not reported.

  5. Corrective action

    Owned through to completion, not escalated back to the client.

  6. Verified closure

    Re-tested before it is closed. Closure returns as new signal.

Operating model

How Xyvionix acts on the evidence

  1. Stage 01

    Baseline

    The outcome is defined in the enterprise’s own terms, thresholds are agreed, and the present condition is measured and recorded.

  2. Stage 02

    Assure

    Evidence is gathered continuously and tested against the thresholds. State is maintained as a current fact rather than a periodic report.

  3. Stage 03

    Correct

    Where assurance is lost, remediation is owned by Xyvionix and carried through to closure that is verified, not asserted.

  4. Stage 04

    Improve

    Thresholds are raised as the business requires more, and the causes of recurring loss are engineered out rather than re-remediated.

Security and trust

Assurance requires access. Access requires proof of its own.

Xyvionix holds the same standard it applies to its clients. The posture is published so security and procurement can evaluate it before a conversation begins.

Trust and assurance overview
Bounded access
Read-only by standing default. Elevation granted per approved change, time-bound and recorded.
Data residency
Enterprise data remains in the enterprise’s own jurisdiction and infrastructure.
Evidence trail
Every action taken under assurance is attributable, retained and available for examination.
Attestation
A SOC 2 readiness programme is planned; ISO 27001 to follow. Status confirmed in writing before any engagement.
Continuity
A documented transition provision in every engagement. Nothing is held that cannot be handed back.
